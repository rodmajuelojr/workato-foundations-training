What you'll build

Two recipes, no code required

You'll build both recipes end-to-end during the session — connecting Salesforce, Xero, and Gmail without writing a line of code.

Accounts Receivable · Deal-to-Invoice
  1. A deal is marked Closed Won in Salesforce
  2. Workato creates a matching draft invoice in Xero
  3. Finance reviews the draft — nothing sends to the customer automatically
Accounts Payable · Inbox-to-Approval
  1. A vendor invoice email lands in Gmail
  2. Workato creates a bill in Xero, awaiting approval
  3. A Salesforce task is created to route it for approval
