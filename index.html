Why these four accounts

Your two flows for the day

The session covers two connected workflows across Gmail, Xero, and Salesforce. The exercises will be built during the session.

Accounts Payable
  1. An invoice is received in Gmail
  2. Invoice data is processed in Xero
  3. The vendor record is created or verified
Accounts Receivable
  1. An invoice is created in Xero
  2. he customer or opportunity is verified in Salesforce
  3. The relevant record is updated

Before you ask

Questions & who to contact

I signed up for Xero weeks ago — do I need to redo it?

If it's been more than 30 days, yes — demo tenants reset on that cycle. Sign up again a day or two before the session so it's fresh.

My Salesforce Developer Edition still hasn't shown up

Email verification can take anywhere from a few minutes to a few hours. Sign up at least a day ahead so there's time to spare.

Do I need to install anything?

No installs — everything runs in the browser. A laptop and a stable connection is all you need.

What if a signup fails the morning of the session?

Reach out before you arrive — see below. We'd rather fix it beforehand than mid-session.
